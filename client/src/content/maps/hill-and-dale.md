## Map Overview

**Style:** Land, very closed, ring of mountains/forests around bases → elevated plateaus + central valley

Players spawn on high ground with a contested valley below. Natural choke points, dense treelines and mountain ridges create **few, narrow approaches** into each plateau. **No water.**

**Map Features:** **5 Relics** (1 near each player + 3 toward center) • **3 Sacred Sites** (usually central, spawn can favor a side) • **2 Trade Posts** (one per side lane, closer to each player's half than true center)

---

## Key Resources

* **Sheep:** 28 / 36 / 54 / 72
* **Berry:** Large 4–10 depending on size; 2 medium / player
* **Deer:** 2 large / player **(1 herd spawns inside/adjacent to each base)**
* **Boars:** 2–5
* **Gold:** Large 11–29; 1 small / player
  **→ One safe 4,000g vein in/near each base; most 8,000g veins cluster in the central valley.**
* **Stone:** Large 4–10; Small 6–20
  **→ One safe stone node in/near each base; others scattered, often side lanes.**
* **Wood:** Abundant. One starting woodline behind/adjacent to base; additional dense lines on side lanes and some in the valley.

**Notes on generation**

* Sacred Sites: generally mid; exact placement can skew slightly toward one player due to mountains. Treat site control as a viable win path when spawns favor you.
* Relics: fairly even overall—1 each side-closer, 3 central. Plan to fight for mid relics rather than relying on back relics only.
* Food: besides your starting berries, **a deer herd spawns safely**; additional deer and boar are usually on side lanes.
* Sheep: fairly even overall, with a **slight mid bias.**

---

## Scouting Pattern (reliable)

1. Send initial scout straight **toward mid** to sweep the sheep-rich spine.
2. If you **meet enemy scout**, pivot to complete **your near side** arc back home.
3. If you **don't** meet them, **cut across to their side** (outside their plateau) and then loop home.
   → This covers mid-first (highest payoff) without overcommitting to one barren flank.

**Pros Scout?** Generally **low priority** here: you already have a safe deer pack in base, and your opponent does too. If you tech Pros Scout early, you're mostly fighting over **two external deer packs** (≈14 deer) which may not justify the investment.

---

## Strategic Themes

* **Closed map → strong defensive macro.** Chokes and wraparound mountains make **few walls** cover your whole side. Mobility is limited; **cavalry flanks are weaker** than on open maps.
* **Mid = Gold bank.** You'll exhaust the **safe 4k gold** quickly; almost all **8k gold** is mid. The player who **stabilizes mid gold** tends to win the long game.
* **Trade is actually feasible.** Posts sit on the outer lanes, a bit back from true center; because the map is closed, **walled trade corridors** are practical.
* **Games skew long.** Wood and gold are plentiful overall; easy turtling pushes games toward **late tech, big armies, siege focus.**

---

## Early Game (I–II)

* **Openers to favor**

  * **Eco-first:** Fast Feudal into **Double TC**, or fast Castle with minimal military if scouting reads passive/open walls.
  * **Trade opener** (if civ supports it): Quick outpost + palisade segments to **seal a lane**, then Merchant production.
* **Walling plan:** Use forests + cliff faces to minimize wall length. One or two short palisade runs can **lock your plateau**. Upgrade to **stone walls** later on key gaps.
* **Sacred pressure option:** If sites spawn favorable to you, **lean into Sacred victory** to punish greedy boom/trade from the opponent.

**Build notes**

* Spend early stone on a **mid outpost/keep timing** if you aim to contest the first central 8k gold. A **tower with Arrow Slits** (or equivalent tech) at a forward gold is often game-deciding.
* **Don't over-index on early cav.** Pathing and chokes reduce raid value; prioritize **archer/spear** or **M@A** to hold space and escort vills staking mid.

---

## Mid–Late Game (III–IV)

* **Secure Mid Gold:** Drop **Keeps** or a chain of **upgraded outposts** on one or two 8k mines; add **towers on ridges** watching the valley approach. Deny opponent's Age-ups/tech.
* **Siege-heavy fights:** Chokes favor **massed infantry + siege** over mobile comps. Trebs break keeps; SPR/Bombards clear towers; Springalds to cover.
* **Stone walls** across the primary valley ramp or narrow passes are cost-effective once your eco peaks. Seal side doors, force frontal pushes.
* **Anti-Trade plan:** Even if you don't trade, **scout lanes**; a few rams + small detachment can delete markets and collapse their economy.

## Civ Fit (Season 11 context)

* **Trade specialists (top picks):**
  **French (Jeanne d'Arc), Ottomans, Abbasid** (when meta allows). These civs can set up **safe, long-distance trade**, and some generate **free/discounted merchants** or economic tech synergies.
* **Late-game/infantry powerhouses:**
  **English** (booming behind walls; landmark pressure with the King; strong farms/archers → late infantry artillery ball), **HRE / Order of the Dragon** variants (fast Castle into M@A floods from safe burgrave-style play), **farm-centric civs** that thrive without map control early.
* **Civs to be cautious with:**
  **Mongols**—strong traders in general, but **lack walls** here is a real liability on a map that rewards lane sealing and static defenses.

---

## Micro & Macro Tips

* **Plateau locks:** Short walls at two or three chokepoints can make you **nearly unraidable.**
* **Vision matters:** Outposts on **ridges** above the ramps spot siege pushes early; add **emplacements** if your civ allows.
* **Push axis discipline:** Once you stonewall side doors, **focus one main lane** with rams/trebs; don't bleed units into forests.
* **Gold denial:** If opponent isn't trading, **deny central gold** with a keep and tower web; you'll delay their Age IV and tech timings dramatically.

---

## Tactics / Notes (quick recap)

* Spawn on high ground plateaus with **limited local gold** (one safe 4k) and **one safe deer pack**.
* Valley holds **most relics (3), most 8k gold, and all Sites** → **center control is decisive** long term.
* Entrances into valley are **natural choke points** → **wall/fortify early**, later upgrade key spans to **stone**.
* Trade Posts on side edges; **contesting enables strong midgame economies.**
* Defensive civs can **lock plateau** and boom; but eventually **must stake mid** for gold.
* Aggressive play that **secures a forward gold** (tower/keep timing) can snowball, even vs. booms.
* **Favor infantry + siege** over pure cav; mobility is constrained.
