## Map Overview

Altai features **three-lane structure** divided by mountain ranges. Each lane typically contains a Sacred Site, making lane control essential for map dominance.  
The map is **fully land-based** (no water), and the combination of **mountains and dense forests** makes it one of the **most closed maps** in rotation — a few short walls can seal off entire lanes.

**Map Features:** 3 Relics (1 per player) • 3 Sacred Sites • 2 Trade Posts

- **Sacred Sites:** One per lane — two near the map edges (each tilted toward one player), and one directly centered.  
- **Trade Posts:** Always two, located **near the left and right edges** of the map. The side they spawn on can vary, but their **edge positioning** makes them **easy to wall and protect** for safe trade routes.

## Key Resources

- **Sheep:** 20 / 36 / 54 / 72 (varies by player count)  
  - Sheep distribution is **uneven across lanes**. You'll usually find **2 behind your TC**, and most of the rest along the **side lanes** — one side often richer (~8) while the other has fewer (~4).  
  - The **center lane** generally has **the least sheep**, so prioritize scouting the flanks for early food.

- **Berry Bushes:** Large 1 per player; Medium 2 per player  
  - The map tends to **favor hunt over berries** — berry density is low compared to other land maps.

- **Deer:** 1v1: 3 small + 1 large per player; Team: 2 large per player  
  - You begin with a **deer patch extremely close** to your Town Center — often right under it.  
  - Most other deer packs on the map are **small (3)** instead of 7, but total deer count is **higher than average**, supporting sustained hunting play.  

- **Boars:** 2 / 3 / 4 / 5  

- **Gold:** Large 2 / 4 / 4 / 5; Small 6 / 8 / 14 / 18  
  - Each player starts with **both gold and stone near their TC**, but **only one large gold vein** close by (usually ~4,000).  
  - Once it's mined out, you must **expand or trade** — running out of safe gold is a major midgame threat on Altai.  

- **Stone:** Large 1 per player; Small 8 / 14 / 20 / 28  

> **Resource Summary:**  
> Safe early economy with food right under your TC, but **limited long-term gold**. The **side lanes** offer rich hunting and sheep opportunities, while the **center** is sparser. Plan expansion early toward outer lanes or trade routes.

---

## Strategic Approach

Map divides into three lanes (center + two flanks) via mountain ranges — Sacred Sites one per lane means lane control equals sacred control.

Altai plays **slow and positional**. Because the map is **closed and chokepointed**, early aggression is risky; it favors **defensive or economic openings**.  
Split pressure across lanes: **contest in lane(s) the opponent neglects**. Fortify **mountain passes** to deny movement and use mobility to rotate.  

**Trade Posts** spawn near "Forgotten Ruins" POIs, typically in **side lanes or mountain passes**. Because resources are fairly even per lane, **lane control** is more important than holding a singular stronghold.

## Strategy Insights (from Season 11)

- **Early Game:** Focus on **eco stability**; walls are cheap and powerful here.  
- **Mid Game:** With limited flanking paths, **siege units** (mangonels, springalds, rams) dominate narrow lanes.  
- **Late Game:** **Trade or relic play** becomes critical once first gold runs dry.

### Recommended Civilizations
- **Top Picks:**  
  - **Ayubids, Chinese (Zhu Xi), Lancaster English:** excel with siege in tight corridors.  
  - **Abbasid, French:** strong **trade potential** thanks to easy-to-protect edges.  
  - **Chinese, English:** ideal for **multi-TC or defensive scaling**.

- **Weaker Picks:**  
  - **Delhi, Templar-style sacred rush civs:** struggle to sustain map control due to chokepoints and limited mobility.

## TL;DR
- **Closed three-lane land map** — walls and choke control are key.  
- **Only one safe gold** → plan trade or relic access.  
- **Side lanes = best sheep & hunt; mid = sparse.**  
- **Siege thrives**; mobility struggles.  
- **Eco or trade civs** outperform early rushers.
