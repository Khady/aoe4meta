## Map Overview

**Style:** Hybrid (land + water) – a canal fully bisects the map

Large rush distance and a very open layout make Canal a slow-to-pressure, hard-to-wall hybrid. Water exists, but it's **shoreline fish only** (villagers can gather; they **do not regenerate**). Naval presence is still impactful for denial and sniping across the canal.

**Map Features:** 5 Relics • 4 Sacred Sites (N/S edges + two mid, one leaning toward each player) • 2 Neutral Trade Posts (near the N/S Sacred Sites)

---

## Key Resources

* **Starting:** 2 nearby Golds ("nearby" is relative), 1 rear Woodline, 1 Berry pack at TC, small Stone, plus **many extra Sheep near your base**
* **Sheep:** Abundant around each spawn (commonly 8–14 close to TC); very sparse in the center
* **Deer:** Fewer than standard; much of the safe food is from sheep/shore fish
* **Boars:** Exactly **2** per map; placement is often asymmetric/awkward (not guaranteed center)
* **Wood:** Generally one good back woodline; additional wood is spread out and exposed
* **Fish:** **Shoreline fish only** along the canal (villagers gather; nodes do not respawn)
* **Trade:** 2 neutral posts, roughly aligned with the north/south Sacred Sites; routes are long and exposed
* **Stone (small):** 8 / 12 / 19 / 24

> **Practical takeaway:** Because forward safe resources are scarce and the map is open, early eco that stays mostly **inside your base** (sheep, farms, shoreline fish) is highly valuable.

---

## Strategy Notes

* **Distance matters.** The map is *huge*. Pure infantry pressure is inefficient; mobility is king. Favor **horsemen/knights**, mobile ranged (e.g., mounted options), and avoid clunky early **siege**.
* **Two core openers:**

  1. **Dock First** for extra food via shoreline fish. Transition to early naval poke (Archer Ship) to deny opponent's dock/fish.
  2. **Pike Rush vs Dock**: If you scout an enemy Dock at Age I, a fast **spearman flood** can burn it before payoff.
* **Age II timing punish:** Skip Dock at Age I, **fast Feudal**, then drop a Dock during age-up and immediately pressure with **early Archer Ship** while you're ahead on tech.
* **Map control vs safety:** You'll eventually need contested wood/gold. Use mobility to **escort villagers** or wall pockets around exposed nodes. Don't over-invest in slow pushes.
* **Sacred pressure is spread.** With 4 sites and long distances, one-player locks are rare; plan for **split defense** and **raiding** rather than turtling on a single choke.

## Civ Picks & Tips

* **Japanese**: Cheap Fishing Boats; **Shinobi** can snipe Docks if the enemy invests in water. If they *don't* dock, **Kura Storehouse** grants safe in-base farming to bridge early food. Pair **heavy cav** with fast anti-cav ranged (e.g., mounted options) for mobile control. Avoid slow infantry cores.

* **Mongols**: Elite at Day 0 adaptation. Use Ovoo stone for **double Spearman** to deny enemy Dock, or if no enemy Dock, **Dock yourself**. Later **Keshigs/Mangudai** excel at map control. Rally **Yam networks** for speed and leverage flexible **Trade** on long lanes.

* **Abbasid**: Dock costs **75 Wood**, enabling early water. Standard plan: quick **Feudal (Military Wing)** → instant **Battering Ram** (no Blacksmith/tech needed) to delete enemy Dock; or, if no enemy Dock, take water safely and poke with an Archer Ship.

* **Ayyubids**: Also **75 Wood** Docks. Strong **fast age** via **Advanced Culture Wing**, then pressure enemy Dock or pivot to water yourself. **Desert Raiders** give good mobility at Age II and scale into potent **heavy cav** at Age III.

* **French & Jeanne d'Arc**: Docks provide **+10 Population**, effectively saving 50 Wood on a House. Pick **School of Cavalry** for immediate **Age II Knights** pressure, or **Chamber of Commerce** to lean into the long, exposed trade lines.

* **Also viable**: **Templar**, **Chinese**, **Zhu Xi**, **Rus**—either via strong early **pike pressure** or simply excellent eco that converts shoreline fish + sheep into a fast tech/raid game. (They're strong in the current meta even without Canal-specific perks.)

## Scouting & Micro

* **Early scout pattern:** Sweep tightly around your base first to scoop the **nearby sheep cluster**, then check the enemy **Dock** status.
* **Naval harassment:** One or two **Archer Ships** can deny multiple shoreline patches due to tight canal geometry; guard them and avoid overextending.
* **Villager safety:** Favor **in-base farms** once sheep thin out; only move out for exposed nodes when you can screen with mobile units.

---

## Quick Build Ideas (1v1)

* **Dock First Safe Boom**: 6–8 on food (sheep) → 3–4 on wood → Dock → 2–3 Fishing Boats → Age II → 1–2 Archer Ships to protect/deny.
* **Scout-into-Pike**: If enemy Dock spotted: early Barracks → constant Spearmen → rally across (waypoint to Dock) → torch it; behind, take your own Dock or fast Age II.
* **Fast Feudal Punish**: No Dock at Age I → Age II quickly → drop Dock during age-up → first unit **Archer Ship** to hit their fishing before they can respond.

---

## TL;DR Gameplan

1. **Decide water vs punish** based on scouting.
2. **Prioritize mobility**; avoid slow siege blobs.
3. **Eco in-base** as long as you can; then **escort** your expansions.
4. Contest **split Sacred Sites** opportunistically; don't chase full map lock.
