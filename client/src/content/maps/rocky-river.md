## Map Overview

Rocky River is a **hybrid-looking, mostly land-play map**: there are ponds, but fish are scarce shore fish that **do not regenerate**, so early docks are niche. The dried riverbed through mid is still the most contested zone with clustered resources and key control points.

**Map Features:** 3 Relics (1 per player) • 3 Sacred Sites • 2 Trade Posts

### Layout & Openness

- **Very large map.** Long rush distances buy time for eco and tech.
- **Base ring = relatively closed.** Expect ~3 nearby woodlines forming natural chokes around each TC; easy to wall/tower your immediate area.
- **Beyond your base = open.** Outer food (deer/boar/berries) spawns tend to be **far** from TC and exposed.
- **Mid can be segmented by water pockets.** You can anchor partial walls between ponds later; doing this very early is risky due to villager walking time.

### Water & Fish (practical impact)

- Ponds hold **few shore fish** (non-regenerating). They're useful as a small early boost or for tactical wall-anchors, but don't expect a true water boom.

## Key Resources

- **Sheep:** 34 / 62 / 92 / 122 (varies by player count)
- **Berry Bushes:** Large 1 per player; Medium 4 per player  
  - **Generation note:** The **starting berry pack can be 6 or 8 bushes** (250 food per bush). Outer berry packs skew toward the **middle of the map**.
- **Deer:** Large 1 per player; Small 8 / 8 / 10 / 14
- **Boars:** 2 / 3 / 4 / 5
- **Fish:** Shore fish from ponds; each pond has 2–3 in large games **and they do not regenerate**
- **Gold:** Large 2 / 4 / 5 / 6; Small 4 per player  
  - **Spawn note:** Front/back distribution varies; one spawn can have safer back golds while the other has more forward golds.
- **Stone:** Large 1 per player; Small 3 per player
- **Forests (extra):** ~3 close **blocking** woodlines per player; **sparser** tree lines as you move outward.

## Relics, Sacred Sites & Trade

- **Sacred Sites:** Always in a **quincunx** pattern with one **dead-center**. The two side Sites sit closer to each player; **one of them is backed by a small mountain**, making it easier to defend. Contesting **all three** is notably hard.
- **Relics:** Three, generally near the **middle**; expect one slightly closer to each side and one more neutral.
- **Trade:** 2 neutral posts exist, but **early trade is difficult**—far from base and the outer map is open. **Mid-game** trade becomes viable if you **secure mid with walls** anchored on ponds.

## Scouting & Sheep Routing

Sheep are **abundant** and help offset how far your natural food is from TC. Expect roughly **~10 on your side**, ~10 on the opponent's side, plus a few in mid (easily **2,000+ food** in total if you scout well).

**Suggested scout path:**
1. Go **straight to mid**.
2. If you **don't** meet the enemy scout there, **dip into their side** briefly to tag extra sheep, then rotate home for leftover sheep around your TC.
3. If you **do** meet them in mid, respect the contest and sweep **your side → home**.

## Strategic Approach

- **Stay safe, scale, then strike.** Because the map is **huge** and outer food is **exposed**, prefer game plans that **keep villagers at home**:
  - **Fast Castle (III)** or **double TC**,  
  - **Farm transition**, **Pro Scouts**, or civ-specific **in-base economy** (e.g., cattle/cows).
- **Mobility > slow pushes.** Long distances make **cavalry** pressure and defense rotations more practical than slow infantry/siege marches.
  - **Early siege** trundling across the map is inefficient; use it **defensively** or **locally** (e.g., vs. forward towers).
- **Mid control timing.** Don't overinvest to **wall mid** in early Feudal—villager walk time is punishing and easy to punish. Once you're ahead or stabilized, **use ponds as wall anchors** to lock the riverbed and convert that into Sacred/Trade control.

## Civ Picks That Shine Here

- **Top pick:** **Malians** — exceptional **in-base eco** (cattle/cow boom) and **mobile cavalry** (Sofas) to punish exposed outer gathers and deny opponent's map take.
- **Also strong:**  
  - **Rus** — **Pro Scouts** + fast III; good cav roster to leverage the map's size.  
  - **English (incl. Lancaster)** — **double TC → farms**; fortress-defense around natural chokes suits them.  
  - **Ottomans** — time to set up military schools and scale into strong Castle timings.  
  - **Japanese** — comfortable **single-TC Fast Castle → farm web** around base.  
  - **Byzantines** — solid **at-home eco**; can take 1–2 nearby berry packs with boosted **Cistern** coverage.  
  - **Chinese (Zhu Xi)** — stable teching, then macro up safely and hit strong Castle power spikes.
- **Delhi** can work for **early mid walls + two Sites**, but expecting **all three Sites** is ambitious due to the protected enemy-side Site.

## Practical Tips

- **Outer food is bait.** If you push out early for far deer/boar, bring **outposts** and expect **cav raids**.
- **Sheep first, farms next.** Use abundant sheep to bridge to farms instead of overextending for mid berries early.
- **Use your natural chokes.** Those 3 base woodlines are perfect for **short, cheap walls** and arrow slits to deter early harassment.
- **Convert mid control into win conditions.** Once stable, wall segments between ponds to secure **center Sacred** and open **safe trade** later.
