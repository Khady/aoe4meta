## Map Overview

**Style:** Land, semi-open

Dry Arabia (Arabie Aride) is a **land-only map** with no water features. While traditionally known as a fully open map, it now plays as a *semi-open* one: each player usually spawns with **two nearby woodlines** that allow partial walling and early defense. This makes it slightly less exposed than before, though mobility and map control remain key.

**Map Features:** 3 Relics (1 per player) • 3 Sacred Sites • 2 Trade Posts (spawn positions vary)

## Key Resources

* **Sheep:** 20 / 36 / 54 / 72 (randomly distributed, often uneven)
* **Berry:** Large 1 / player; Medium 2 / player
* **Deer:** 2 large / player (≈14 deer total per side)
* **Boars:** 2 / 3 / 4 / 5
* **Gold:** Large 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 (one close to TC, one mid); Small 8 / 14 / 20 / 27
* **Stone:** Large 2 / 4 / 6 / 8 (one close to TC); Small 8 / 14 / 21 / 28

### Resource Notes

* Abundant gold and stone make **double TC** or **early aggression** viable.
* **Deer packs** are large and easy to pull under TC — great for **Pro Scouts** openings.
* **Sheep spawns** are highly random and sometimes unfairly distributed; strong scouting is critical.

## Map Characteristics

* **Terrain:** Flat with scattered hills and forests.
* **Woodlines:** Two near each TC, usable for partial walls or defensive structures.
* **Trade Posts:** Spawn positions are **semi-random** — can be behind your base, on the edge, or central. Trade is **not always viable** depending on spawn.

## Scouting

* Start by sending your scout **toward the map center**, then sweep outward in a semicircle.
* If you **meet the enemy scout early**, don't chase — turn back and cover your side to avoid losing all sheep.
* Prioritize identifying **neutral market positions** and **safe deer packs**.
* If playing as **Rus**, consider a **second scout** for faster deer collection.

## Tactics / Strategy

* **Map control** is everything — gold and stone clusters gravitate toward the middle.
* **Pro Scouts** is one of the strongest openings here; it leverages the easy deer access and strong early eco.
* **Walls** can be used early between your two woodlines to secure your base.
* **Aggressive playstyles** thrive; expect raids from multiple angles.
* **Trade** can be situationally strong if the neutral market spawns safely behind your base.

### Economic Plans

* **Safe Trade:** Only if the post spawns defensively; best for Mongols or French.
* **Double TC:** Risky — the map's openness makes greed punishable.
* **Pro Scouts:** High reward; especially strong for Rus, Chinese, or Jeanne d'Arc.

## Best Civilizations

* **Top Tier:** Rus, Chinese, Jeanne d'Arc (for Pro Scouts)
* **Strong Aggressors:** French, Mongols, Delhi Sultanate (sacred site pressure)
* **Defensive Picks:** Byzantines can hold, but greedy civs like **Abbasid** or **English** struggle due to pressure and exposed expansions.

## Key Takeaways

* Semi-open terrain = flexible defense, but don't rely on full walling.
* Scout smartly; sheep RNG can decide early eco tempo.
* Contest mid golds and sacred sites — whoever controls center controls the game.
* Expect early pressure: **build fast, fight faster**.
