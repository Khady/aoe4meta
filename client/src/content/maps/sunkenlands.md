## Map Overview

Sunkenlands is **100% land** and plays as a **very open map**: few natural chokepoints, scattered cliffs/mountains, and plenty of nearby forests for early walling or arrow-slits but, overall, lots of room for raids. Spawns still create "sunk" craters around TCs—expand outward rather than inward.

Central objectives are tightly grouped and equidistant:
- **Sacred Sites:** 2 total, both near the middle (left/right of center), same distance from each player.
- **Relics:** 3 total with a classic layout—one favoring each side and one centralish.
- **Trade Posts:** 2, located **in the middle lane**. They're effectively non-viable for trade routes and hard to contest even late.

**Takeaway:** Early **center control** remains vital for relics/sites, but the map's openness strongly rewards **mobile armies** and quick pivots.

## Key Resources

- **Sheep:** 30 / 36 / 54 / 72 (varies by player count)  
  _Placement notes:_ Sheep are spread fairly evenly **left/right and mid** (roughly ~5 in the center). A practical scouting route is to **pick a side**, sweep outward past a Sacred Site, then loop back toward your base. Only go dead-center if you must—side sweeps are more efficient.

- **Berry:** Large 1 per player; Medium 1 per player + 4 contested  
  _Placement notes:_ Expect an **8-berry patch near your base**, plus additional berry packs toward the left/right flanks. Side packs are tempting but far—exposed without fortification.

- **Deer:** Large up to 8 (varies); Medium 2 per player + 2 contested  
  _Placement notes:_ Typically **two 5-deer herds very close to each base** (excellent Pro Scouts value), plus **~5 deer on each side**. Contest the side herds; stealing the opponent's close herds is usually unrealistic unless you already have map control.

- **Boars:** 2 / 5 / 6 / 7  
  _Placement notes:_ Normally **one boar per side** (left/right). Very rarely one side can **lack a boar**—plan flexible food options.

- **Gold:** Large 0 / 4 / 4 / 5; Small 6 / 10 / 14 / 19; Tiny 2 per player  
  _Placement notes:_ Near each base you'll usually find **two 1600 golds (not under the TC)** and a **4000 gold not too far**, with **~3 additional mines leaning toward mid**. **Gold is scarce overall** and the first 1600s are pressure magnets. After those are gone, remaining mines are far and risky.

- **Stone:** Large 0 / 4 / 6 / 8; Small 7 / 14 / 21 / 28  
  _Placement notes:_ Expect a **1200 stone near base**; the rest skews outward. Consider early outposts/keeps to secure mid resources if your plan needs stone.

## Strategic Approach

**1) Play the gold game first.**  
Gold is the limiting factor. Either:
- **Pressure** the enemy's forward 1600s (early outpost/tower denial, cavalry raids), or  
- **Defend** yours with walls/outposts, then **leapfrog** to a mid mine behind a keep.

**2) Lean mobile.**  
The map's openness heavily favors **cavalry** and multi-prong raiding over slow infantry+siege balls. If you go infantry/siege, you'll need superior vision, walls, and forward fortifications.

**3) Pro Scouts is legit here.**  
With **10 deer close to home** and **~10 more on the flanks**, Pro Scouts has great ROI. Contest the side herds; don't overinvest trying to yoink the opponent's close packs unless you already control the area.

**4) Fast Castle is often viable.**  
Defensive terrain around your crater plus nearby forests make early shoring-up reasonable. Hitting Castle gives you **heavy cav** and **better counters**, or access to **keeps** to lock **mid gold + sacreds**. Pair this with **relic play** to offset gold scarcity.

**5) Be realistic about side food.**  
The left/right clusters (boar/berries/deer) look juicy but are **far and exposed**. If you must take them, **go with a small crew, drop an upgraded outpost (or a keep)**, and pre-plan walls. Otherwise, prefer safe farms at home once your early hunts run out.

**6) Trading's a trap.**  
The posts are in the **middle lane** with no safe corner-to-corner path. Treat trade as **effectively off** for standard play.

## Civ Considerations

- **Resource-flex / passive-gold / Pro Scouts champs:**  
  - **Rus:** Bounty economy + strong Pro Scouts + mobile pressure.  
  - **Zhu Xi's Legacy / Chinese:** Taxes (passive trickle), solid defensive landmarks (e.g., **Barbican**) to anchor a gold.  
  - **Ottomans:** **Military Schools** ease unit production while you eco/tech; **fast Castle + relics** smooth out gold.  
  - **English:** Farm economy turns on quickly; can anchor mid with **White Tower/keeps** and grind out longer games.  
  - **Japanese:** Smooth age-ups; stone/gold synergy supports defensive **tower springalds** to secure forward mines, then transition into strong cav/ranged.

- **Less comfortable here:**  
  - **Sacred-site-centric openers** (e.g., Delhi-style rushes) struggle because sites are **smack in the middle** and there are only **two**—if you lose mid control, the win condition evaporates.  
  - **Static, infantry-heavy plans** that need wide map access for resources; you can get bled by cavalry and choked off the mid mines.

## Practical Openers & Plays

- **Cav pressure into tower denial:** Harass the nearest 1600 gold, force idle time, drop an outpost to anchor a rally point; pivot to the other side with your mobility.  
- **Fast Castle → Relics/Keep:** Tech up behind light walls, grab **1–2 relics**, and **plant a keep** covering a mid gold plus a Sacred Site.  
- **Pro Scouts timing:** Pull your close herds first (fast & safe), then contest one flank herd while your army screens.  
- **Home-eco fallback:** If the flanks get too hot or a boar is missing on your side, swap to **farms** sooner rather than bleeding vils in the open.
