## Map Overview

Carmel is an **open, very large land map** with minor elevation and rock features. Bases spawn roughly toward the middle third horizontally, leaving **long side lanes**. Gold is scarce and front-loaded at home; **one massive 8,000 gold vein spawns at dead center**, making map control, relics, and (situational) trade the backbone of mid-game economy.

**Map Features:** **5 Relics (all mid-oriented)** • **2 Sacred Sites** (far North/South, hard to hold) • ~1 Trade Post per player (near Capital, short route)

## Key Resources

* **Sheep:** 34 / 36 / 54 / 72 (varies by player count)
  *Spawn pattern:* concentrated along the flanks; expect ~15 on "your" half in 1v1, rewarding a fast two-scout opener.
* **Berry:** 6 medium per player
  *Notes:* one patch is near TC; multiple extra patches fan out toward the sides.
* **Deer:** Extra large 2 per player; small 3 per player
  *Notes:* typically **3 deer close to TC** plus **large herds further out**, fueling fast Castle timings with Pro Scouts.
* **Boars:** 2 per player
* **Gold:** Large 1; Tiny 13 / 22 / 31 / 40
  *Crucial clarifications:* each player gets **two small 1,600g nodes near TC (one often directly under TC, very safe)**; **all other small map nodes are also 1,600g**, **except** the **central 8,000g "king vein."**
* **Stone:** Small 6 / 10 / 14 / 20
  *Notes:* ~1,200 stone near TC; additional stone is much farther and riskier.

## Scouting & Openers

* **Two-Scout start is high value.** Run one straight mid, the other to your close flank; if you meet the enemy scout, mirror back to "your" side. Sheep and deer are spread wide, especially along the edges.
* **Pro Scouts is premium** here: deer/herds and boars are plentiful but exposed. Hauling food back to TC centralizes eco and blunts cavalry raids.

## Strategic Approach

* **Early denial on gold is weak:** with a **1,600g node under the TC**, early "pike-rush/torch deny" plays don't bite. You'll reliably get the gold needed for **fast Castle (Age III)**.
* **Game plan template (1v1):**

  1. Two scouts → **Pro Scouts** (or double-scout + fast Castle if your civ can skip Pro Scouts).
  2. **Hit Castle quickly** off abundant food + safe home gold.
  3. **Plant a Keep (or landmark/tower) at mid** to secure the **8,000g central vein**.
  4. **Snag relics** (they're all mid-biased) and contest Sacred Sites only if you already control mid.
  5. If opponent locks mid with a Keep, **build trebuchets** promptly; without that center gold, your tech/army quality will stall.
* **Unit theme:** the map's **size + openness → cavalry shines**. Aim for mobile comps (heavy cav, horse archers) over slow infantry/siege balls early. Infantry can come later to anchor mid once gold is secured.
* **Trade:** each player has a neutral post "behind" base but the **route is short (mid-board),** so raw gold/second is mediocre and hard to protect. It's more **situational/power-spike** (e.g., mercenaries) than a primary economy.

## Sacred Sites & Relics

* **2 Sacred Sites** sit **far North/South**, away from common mid armies; they're tough to capture **and** to defend.
* **5 Relics** spawn **evenly toward mid** (none at home). Given the map's **overall gold squeeze**, **relic income is pivotal** after your initial home nodes run out.

## Civ Recommendations

* **S-Tier:** **Rus** — passive gold from hunting cabins and on-hunt bounty synergizes with Carmel's gold squeeze; great **Pro Scouts**, mobile rosters, fast relics with warrior monks.
* **A-Tier:**

  * **Zhu Xi's Legacy** — excellent Pro Scouts; strong **heavy cav** spikes (forge techs incl. +20% cav attack speed) to dominate open maps.
  * **Chinese** — can be slower/more infantry-leaning pre-Castle, but **Barbican at mid** after Castle is a **cheap, sturdy mid anchor**, and **Nest of Bees** later hold chokes.
* **B+ Tier / Playable strengths:**

  * **French / Jeanne d'Arc** — smooth fast Castle into **knights**; Jeanne's auras and armor techs stack well for mid fights.
  * **Byzantines** — strong eco scaling; **olive oil → mercenaries** via the nearby neutral market can unlock spicy midgame timings; cistern buffs love the plentiful berries.
  * **Japanese** — robust Castle Age with powerful heavy cav and strong farm upgrades; map size gives time to establish eco.
  * **English / House of Lancaster** — niche line: **fast Castle** and drop **White Tower/Tower of London** or a forward Keep on the center vein to force mid control (risk: opponent's food abundance lets them flood if you don't break out).

## Practical Tips

* **Centralize your food** (Pro-scouted deer/boar under TC) to reduce raiding paths.
* **Spend early gold wisely**; once home nodes deplete, **relics + center vein** decide the tech race.
* **Don't over-invest in early infantry**; get mobility first, then add infantry/siege **after** you have mid secured.
* **If you lose mid**, **immediately transition to trebs** and deny repair access; every minute the opponent mines center is a widening tech gap.
