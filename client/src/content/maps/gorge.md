## Map Overview

**Style:** Land, very open with small forests / some elevation

100% land map with varied terrain. Most forests are small (one big woodline sits behind each base); the openness + long distances to outer resources make map control and mobility huge.

**Map Features:** 5 Relics (1 safer per player + 3 central) • 3 Sacred Sites (all mid, equidistant) • 2 Neutral Trade Posts (mid-lane, highly contested)

## Key Resources

- **Sheep:** 26 / 46 / 66 / 86  
  - Usually **~2 near each TC** and the rest split on the flanks. Very few (sometimes none) spawn in the map center.
- **Berry:** Large 1 / player; Medium 2 / player  
  - **Starter:** a 6-bush pack close to your TC.
- **Deer:** Large 2 / player  
  - Often **forward/side** and hard to defend without map control.
- **Boars:** 2 / 3 / 4 / 5
- **Gold:** Large 2 / 7 / 10 / 13; Small 6 / 6 / 8 / 10  
  - **Starter:** a **4,000** gold vein near each TC.  
  - **Side rule:** one flank typically has **8,000 + 4,000**, the opposite flank has **only 4,000**—you'll be forced wide once the starter runs out.
- **Stone:** Large 2 / 4 / 6 / 8; Small 8 / 10 / 15 / 20  
  - **Starter:** a **1,200** stone vein near each TC.
- **Wood:**  
  - A **big back woodline** is comfy early, but as you chop it back your TC stops covering it—expect midgame cav raids.

## Tactics / Notes

- Most contested resources sit on elevated sides; gorge center is more open.
- Center contains a choke that's a bit easier to wall / defend.
- **Trade:** Posts are **in the middle**, not edges—**early trade is basically dead**. Even late it's hard to keep secure.
- **Scouting pattern (huge on this map):**
  - Snag the **2 near-TC sheep** first, then commit your scout to **one flank** (aim roughly along the sacred on that side) and hug the edge.  
  - **Avoid the middle early**—there are usually **0–1 sheep** there and you'll just waste time.
- **Play for mobility & pressure:** wide, open lanes + far-out resources = **cavalry raids shine**. Deny side golds/deer, force idle time.
- **Outpost + Mill deer take:** send **5 vills** to a forward deer pack, drop **Outpost → Mill**. Five can garrison vs early cav and keep the spot safe.
- **Proscoot is S-tier here:** pulling deer under TC simplifies defense (your eco stays under TC/Outpost auras) and frees you to pressure.
- **Sacred control is viable:** 3 equidistant sites mid; use small forests & ridges to make **short wall segments** that slow decaps and buy rotate time. Works especially well vs turtly opponents.

## Strategy Sketch

- **Early:** Fast military production + scout prioritization. Grab near sheep, pick a flank, contest side golds/deer.  
- **Mid:** Expand with screens of Outposts on the sides; keep cavalry rotating. If going sacred, **segment-wall** to delay decaps.  
- **Late:** If you must trade, only after you've won mid control; otherwise it bleeds units.

## Strong Civ Ideas (S11 flavor)

- **Proscoot + mobility:** **Rus, Chinese, Zhu Xi's Legacy, French, Jeanne d'Arc**—great at deer pull + active cav pressure.
- **Sacred/Map control:** **Delhi** (free Pro Scouts; walls + sacred tempo are excellent here).
- **Solid at home, minimal map exposure:** **House of Lancaster, English, Japanese**—farm and boom safely, then move out behind heavy cav/defensive anchors.  
  - (Byzantines can use mid posts for mercs, but the posts being central makes that plan risky.)

## Practical Reminders

- Your **first 4k gold** disappears fast—plan a side move before it's dry.  
- As the **back woodline thins**, your TC stops covering it. Add Outposts or shift camps early to avoid being blindsided.  
- If you see the opponent turtling, **pivot to sacred timing** with light walling; it punishes passive play on Gorge.
