## Map Overview

**Style:** Land, semi-closed starts → open midgame • elevation • (some) stealth forest

Bases spawn "enclaved" behind short cliffs with a narrow, wallable front — very close to the opponent laterally, but with little to raid at 0:00 aside from under-TC gold. Outer resources are far, so midgame map control matters a ton.

**Map Features:** 5 Relics (1v1 standard) • 3 Sacred Sites (clustered together) • 2 (1v1 & 2v2) / 4 Trade Posts (3v3 & 4v4) • Multiple Points of Interest (see below)

### Points of Interest (capture/destroy)
- **Merchant Camps:** 1 near each base. After capture, trickles **+15 Food +15 Wood +15 Gold every 60s** (45 total/min). Hotly contested early.
- **Abandoned Outpost:** Between the two bases. Grants persistent line of sight (doesn't shoot, no garrison).
- **Wolf Den (x1 per side):** *Destroyable*, not capturable. On destroy you immediately gain **+150 Gold**. Spawns 2 strong wolves at ~70% HP and again at ~30% HP; from **5:00** onward, wolves also spawn periodically nearby (up to 5 active). Clearing/handling it is a major early objective.
- **Lost Ronin:** *Capturable*. From **5:00**, stock begins accruing **1 Ronin every 120s** (can bank multiple). Either player can flip and spend its stock.

## Key Resources

- **Sheep:** 26 / 46 / 66 / 86
- **Berry:** Large 1 / player; Medium 2 / player
- **Deer:** 2 large / player
- **Boars:** 2 / 3 / 4 / 5
- **Gold:** Large 4 / 8 / 12 / 14; Small 4 / 4 / 8 / 11; Tiny 2 per player
- **Stone:** Large 2 / 3 / 4 / 5; Small 8 / 14 / 19 / 28

**Spawn tendencies & access notes**
- **Start gold:** Your TC-safe vein is only **1600**; a second vein sits nearby in-basin. Once local veins/berries are spent, you must push out into the open where most remaining gold/stone/boar sit.
- **Sacred Sites:** All **three are clustered** in the central zone and are comparatively easy to hold once you control the area.
- **Relics:** Typical 1v1 spread of **five**: two between bases; the other three split as one closer to you, one mid, one closer to the opponent.

## Scouting & Sheep Pathing

Best opener: send your scout **out the front toward the central corridor** (don't wrap the wrong side). The densest sheep pockets sit **between the two bases and slightly above that lane**. Options after first haul:
1) Sweep **upward along mid→top** to vacuum clusters, then loop home;  
2) If food feels tight, grab near-mid sheep and **deposit early** under TC, then resume;  
3) Greedy/high-risk: continue past mid into **opponent's half** if their scout isn't contesting.

## Early Game Plan (why this map is weird… and great)

- **Spear (Pike) Rush for 150g:** Quick Barracks → **3+ Spearmen** to burn **your Wolf Den** for the instant **+150g** power spike. Use your **scout to body-block/tank** wolf aggro (it regenerates HP; spears don't). **Leave once the 150g triggers**—don't bleed HP finishing the structure.
- **Fold in Merchant Camp:** Plant **Barracks + House on the Camp's capture circle** with the builder so you **auto-cap while building**; first spear finishes the capture before jogging to the Den.
- **Macro shape:** 2 on wood after Rax/House; **majority on food** (sheep) to turn the 150g into an **age-up without mining**. If the opponent mirrors, keep spear production to protect your forward buildings/Camp.

## Midgame Control & Win Conditions

- **Semi-closed into open:** Your enclave is easy to wall, but **all meaningful follow-up resources are far**. If you get **pinned in**, you run dry. If you're ahead, **contain instead of diving**—catch reinforcements in the choke, **deny their push-outs**, and let their base exhaust.
- **Sacred lock:** With Sites **clustered**, a single mid fortification can secure all three for a **win timer** while you contain exits.
- **Trade:** Distances are decent, **but wolves gate the routes**. If a Den still stands, expect elite wolves on paths; even with the 150g taken, extra wolves often linger—**clear them** before you cart up.
- **Ronin timing:** From **5:00**, a steady **1 per 120s** can swing skirmishes. Flip/defend that POI during your contain.
- **Vision:** Keep the **Abandoned Outpost** flipped to spot all mid-moves and backstabs.

## Tactics / Notes

- Players spawn on small cliffs overlooking a central valley; the **front is commonly walled** early.  
- **Sheep-heavy starts**; berries exist but are **not comfortably under TC**, and are awkward to defend vs. early pressure.  
- **Relics cluster around mid + shared lane**; Sacred Sites are **together**—great for a mid fort/monk timing.  
- **POIs to contest:** Merchant Camps • Wolf Dens • Abandoned Outpost • Lost Ronin • (plus neutral Trade Posts).  
- **Abandoned Outpost** = free vision only; **Merchant Camps** = **+45 res/min**; **Wolf Den** = **+150g on burn** with dangerous wolf spawns; **Lost Ronin** = **1 Ronin/120s from 5:00**.  
- Because outer gold/stone/boar are far and elevation breaks lines, **valley control** gives a strong age-2/age-3 bridge to Sites/Relics/Trade denial.

## Civ Notes (1v1 bias)

- **House of Lancaster (English variant):** Safe men-at-arms pressure to **bully Camps**, strong sustain with Abbey of Kings; great for contain → Sites.  
- **Mongols:** Explosive spear mass, mobile contain, fast **age-3 siege** to crack front walls; can pivot to trade once wolves are cleared.  
- **Ottomans:** **90w Barracks** + Imperial Council synergies (food/healing) make the **Den + Camp** opener smooth; Imam healing keeps early spears healthy.  
- **Templar/Order civs** (wood-positive openers): Extra wood tempo makes the spear plan and early structures painless.  
- **Japanese / English (core):** Cheaper Rax (JP) and early MAA (EN) give sturdy **anti-spear mirrors** and stable eco while staying mostly in-base.
